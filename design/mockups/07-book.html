02 · The mark

The unicorn

A hand-drawn head, black outline, pastel mane. It is the house signature — the one element every sub-brand inherits in spirit.

  • R-01Clear space ≥ 1× mark height, all sides.
  • R-02Minimum size 24px digital · 12mm print.
  • R-03Never recolor, outline, stretch, or crop the horn.
  • R-04Prefer the gradient field on dark; mono only in 1-color print.

03 · Color

Purple leads,
cyan answers.

Unicorn Purple does the talking; cyan is the reply. Near-black keeps both honest. Tints are allowed only as gradient passages between the two.

  • R-05Purple : cyan ratio roughly 70:30 in any composition.
  • R-06Gradients travel the hue arc purple→cyan; never detour through red or green.
  • R-07Text sits on near-black or white only — never on mid-tones.
