House rules

The contract every agent signs.
HR-01Propose, don't publish

Default posture. Production state changes need a human or an explicit auto-grant.

HR-02The gate is universal

Every artifact through compliance — human-made, agent-made, or MCP-made. No exceptions, including you.

HR-03Budgets are hard

Per-agent weekly caps, metered at the gateway. An agent that hits its cap stops and says so.

HR-04Everything signs its work

Actor, model, refs, cost on every artifact. Provenance is the permission to act again.
